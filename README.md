# 🧠 Mental Health Survey Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Mental Health Survey Dashboard** created to analyze mental health patterns among individuals based on various factors such as gender, days spent indoors, social weakness, and mood swings.

The dashboard helps in understanding how lifestyle and social behavior may influence mental health conditions.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

* **Total Participants:** Displays the total number of survey respondents.
* **Gender Distribution:** Visual representation of male and female participants.
* **Mental Health History by Country:** A world map showing the distribution of mental health history.
* **Days Indoors Analysis:** Shows how long individuals tend to stay indoors.
* **Social Weakness Analysis:** Identifies whether respondents experience social weakness.
* **Mood Swings Analysis:** Displays the distribution of mood swing levels among participants.

---

## 📈 Key Visualizations

* Donut Charts
* Bar Charts
* Interactive Map
* Filters for Gender and Country

These visualizations help in identifying patterns and trends related to mental health.
